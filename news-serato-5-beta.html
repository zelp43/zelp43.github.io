Serato DJ Pro 5.0 beta is here

The beta for the next major version is open. Worth a look — on your own terms, and not on the machine you are gigging with this week.

Back up first

This is the step people skip. A beta writes to the same crate and database files your working setup depends on, and rolling a Serato version back does not always roll back what it wrote.

Take a copy of your library before you install anything. Selecta Tool handles that side of it — build, export and repair your crate tree so you have a clean snapshot to return to.

Rule of thumb

Test on a spare rig, or on a copy of your library. Keep your known-good install as the one that goes out the door.

Does Selecta Tool still work

Selecta Tool reads and writes the standard Serato library format, so it carries on as normal. If something in the 5.0 beta changes that format in a way that trips it up, tell us — info@selectahub.com or the WhatsApp group — and we will get it handled.

Getting the beta

The build, the system requirements and Serato’s own release notes all come from Serato directly — head to serato.com and take it from there rather than from a mirror.
