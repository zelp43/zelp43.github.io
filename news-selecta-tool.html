Release

Selecta Tool 2.2 is out

Out now on Mac and Windows, at the same version on both. The headline of this one is the Gig Desk: bookings, quotes, invoices and the money behind them, handled inside the app on Pro and Pro Plus. One build covers Basic, Pro and Pro Plus — your licence key decides what it unlocks.

What’s in it

  • The Gig Desk
    A new tab on Pro and Pro Plus for the business side of the work: bookings, quotes, invoices, receipts, performance agreements, and a Money view that tracks what you are owed and what you have been paid.
  • Library building, sorting, exporting and repair across your whole Serato crate tree.
  • Fixes and refinements from the 2.2 development cycle.
  • Mac and Windows shipping in step at this version.

The full change list ships in the README bundled with the download.

The Gig Desk

Log a booking, then raise the paperwork against it — quotes, invoices, receipts and performance agreements, carrying your logo, accent colour and social handles in one of four print styles.

It handles multiple currencies (USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, JMD, TTD, EC$, CI$ or your own), one tax rate plus two labelled extra charges, saved clients, line-ups that credit each performer, and double-booking warnings. The Money view tracks collected, expenses and net profit by month and by currency — including cash taken with no paperwork — and exports a financial report as PDF or CSV. Documents are numbered only when you issue them, and emails open in your own mail app.

Everything lives on your machine in a .selgigs file you can back up or move like any other file. The Gig Desk never touches your Serato library.

Which tiers

Pro and Pro Plus. On Pro Plus it is included on every one of the three seats. Basic is unchanged — the core library workflow is all still there.

Already own it

Nothing to buy and no new key to enter. Your update link is the one that came with your purchase — install the new build over the top and your crates, settings and library data stay exactly where they are. Lost the link? Mail info@selectahub.com and we will send it again.

Moving up a tier

There is no separate installer. Basic, Pro and Pro Plus all run from this same app — open Settings, enter your Pro or Pro Plus key, and the features unlock in place.

New to it

Selecta Tool builds, sorts, exports, fixes and masters your Serato library. The full feature and pricing breakdown is on the Selecta Tool page, and a walkthrough video is on the way.

There is a free trial, briefly

Alongside this release there is a free trial, and it is a very limited one in both senses. It runs for a couple of hours, not days, and inside that window you get limited use of build crates, Live Library and Duplicate Finder. Everything else in the app stays locked.

It is one-time, one machine, one email, and it is open for a limited period rather than as a standing part of the product. It is open now — the link is at the top of the Selecta Tool page, under the hero.
