Frequently Asked Questions

Selecta Tool is a DJ library management app built for Serato DJ Pro. It lets you build and export crates, scan folders, find duplicates, preview tracks, and much more, all outside of Serato. Available for Mac (Intel & Apple Silicon) and Windows 10/11.

Basic covers the core workflow: crate building, scanning, duplicate finding, track preview, and exporting. Pro adds power-user features: Library Backup & Restore, File Doctor, Tag Editor, Library Snapshots, Cue Studio, the Sampler, the Gig Desk for bookings and invoicing, and more. Both tiers ship under the same installer.

Pro Plus includes everything in Basic and Pro, the Gig Desk included on every seat, plus a 3-seat license: install on up to 3 machines or share with up to 3 users. It also adds seat revoke & transfer (up to 3× within 30 days), the ability to purchase extra seats at the intro price for up to 60 days, WhatsApp & SMS direct support, exclusive special genre crates, a Sound FX bundle, and early beta access. It's built for DJs who work across multiple setups, run a sound system, or want the best value across a crew. Pro Plus is available on both Mac and Windows.

The Gig Desk is a Pro and Pro Plus tab that handles the business side of DJing from inside Selecta Tool. Log your bookings, then raise quotes, invoices, receipts and performance agreements against them, branded with your logo, accent colour and social handles in one of four print styles. It handles multiple currencies (USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, JMD, TTD, EC$, CI$ or your own), one tax rate plus two labelled extra charges, saved clients, line-ups that credit each performer, and double-booking warnings. The Money view tracks collected, expenses and net profit by month and currency, including cash taken with no paperwork, and exports a financial report PDF or a CSV. Documents are numbered only when you issue them, emails open in your own mail app, and everything is stored on your machine in a .selgigs file you can back up or move. The Gig Desk never touches your Serato library.

Upgrading from Basic to Pro?

No new installer, no redownload. All three tiers run from the same app, your license key is what unlocks the features. Basic users upgrade directly inside the app: go to Settings and enter your Pro or Pro Plus license key. Your crates and library data stay exactly as they are.

Multi-seat & corporate licenses
